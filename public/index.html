Whether it’s a recent sports injury, chronic pain condition, stress, trouble sleeping, lung diseases, fertility, hormone balance, digestive issues, we are here to help. TCMFIT aims to help restore balance to the mind and body.

TCMFIT accepts many types of health insurance plans, including Kaiser, Blue Shield, HealthNet, Aetna, Anthem, Cigna, Medrisk, and more. Please obtain a PCP referral with a covered diagnostic code to guarantee insurance coverage for acupuncture service if insurance requires. Insurance usually will only cover acupuncture for treating the symptoms listed in your referral letter. Please send the following information ONLY to info@tcmfit.com for the insurance eligibility check before booking your acupuncture with insurance initial appointment (or you will need to self-pay for the session).

  • Photos of the front and back of your insurance card
  • Patient’s date of birth
  • Primary care physician referral letter (photocopy if required)

For follow-up appointments, please fill the forms listed below before you come:

About Ting Xie, OMD PHD LAC

Dr. Ting Xie received her Bachelor of Medicine degree (OMD) from the prestigious Beijing University of Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) in China. Following her hospital externship at the Beijing Hospital of Traditional Chinese Medicine, Dr. Xie pursued a PhD at the same university. Upon earning her PhD, she became a licensed acupuncturist and herbalist of Traditional Chinese Medicine. Dr. Xie has extensive experience as an online TCM practitioner and has conducted research on pulmonary diseases at medical centers in the USA. She specializes in treating pulmonary, digestive, cardiovascular, and bone disorders, as well as women’s health issues, using acupuncture and traditional Chinese medicine. Additionally, she has authored numerous medical research papers during her tenure in medical centers. Her comprehensive education in both TCM and Western medicine provides her with a unique holistic approach to patient care.
